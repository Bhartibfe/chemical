## SEO standard

- Unique `<title>` under 60 characters and `meta description` under 155, each
  carrying a real search term rather than filler.
- Exactly one `<h1>`; `h2` and `h3` form a true outline. Heading level is never
  a styling decision.
- Canonical URL, Open Graph, Twitter card, `geo.region` (IN-PB), `geo.placename`.
- Descriptive `alt` on every image; meaningful link text, never "click here".
- JSON-LD: the site ships `Organization` — keep it and extend with
  `LocalBusiness` (both addresses, `areaServed`, hours), `Product` for the
  chemical catalog, `BreadcrumbList`, and `FAQPage`. Validate before shipping.
- Target the searches buyers actually run: "sulphuric acid supplier Punjab",
  "caustic soda flakes manufacturer India", "boiler water treatment chemicals
  Chandigarh" — chemical plus role plus place. Long-tail beats head terms here.
- Technical hygiene: crawlable HTML rather than JS-only rendering, sitemap,
  robots.txt, fast LCP, no orphan pages.

## GEO/AEO standard — how AI engines quote you

An AI engine cites what it can lift cleanly. Write for extraction:

1. **Answer first.** Open every section with a complete, standalone sentence
   that answers the implied question. A quoted fragment must make sense with
   zero surrounding context.
2. **Name entities explicitly, every time.** "SHIV ENTERPRISES supplies sodium
   hypochlorite for municipal water treatment across Punjab" — never "we supply
   it to clients in the region".
3. **Specific and checkable beats promotional.** "ISO 9001:2015 certified, 26+
   chemical products, based in Sardulgarh, Punjab" outperforms "leading
   world-class supplier". Strip unverifiable superlatives.
4. **Structure for parsing.** Short paragraphs, question-and-answer phrasing
   that mirrors real queries, comparison tables, and definition-style openers
   ("Poly aluminium chloride is a coagulant used to…").
5. **Consistency across the corpus.** The same claim, phrased the same way, in
   the copy, the JSON-LD, and the meta description compounds confidence.
6. **Add an FAQ block** answering genuine procurement questions — minimum order,
   delivery areas, grades, documentation, lead time. These are exactly the
   questions AI engines get asked, and `FAQPage` schema makes them liftable.

## Working rules

- Additive: rewrite the weakest copy, not the whole page. Never overwrite
  `weeebbbb.html`.
- Keep the existing brand voice — technical, direct, credible. No hype and no
  keyword stuffing; stuffing hurts rankings now and reads as spam to LLMs.
- When you change copy, state the target query and why the new wording wins.
- Ask before altering business facts, client names, or claimed certifications.
